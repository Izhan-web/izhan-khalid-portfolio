# Project Brief — Izhan's Portfolio Landing Page

## Purpose
Build a simple personal portfolio landing page that introduces Izhan, communicates his academic background and technical interests, and makes it easy to get in touch.

## Audience
Recruiters, internship coordinators, fellow students, and people interested in connecting about technology.

## Page structure
Keep the page short and focused:

### 1. Header
- Wordmark: `IZHAN`
- Navigation: Home, About, Interests, Contact
- Each item must link to a real section on the page.
- Keep the header compact and visually quiet.

### 2. Hero
- Small role label: `COMPUTER SCIENCE STUDENT`
- Main heading: `Hi, I'm Izhan.`
- Supporting headline: `Exploring technology. Building solutions.`
- Short introduction:
  `I'm a Computer Science student interested in networking, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and software development. I enjoy learning new technologies and applying my knowledge through practical work.`
- Include one clear, useful contact link or scroll link. Do not create multiple competing calls to action.
- Use a text-led layout. No profile photo.

### 3. About
Use a short paragraph, not a long biography:
`I'm currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science at COMSATS University Islamabad. I'm interested in understanding how technology works, exploring different areas of computing, and developing practical technical knowledge.`

### 4. Interests
Show these as a compact typographic list or restrained grid:
- Networking
- Cybersecurity
- Artificial Intelligence
- Software Development
- Web Development

This is not a skills-rating section. Do not add percentages, proficiency bars, or unsupported skill claims.

### 5. Contact
A short invitation to connect. Use only real destinations supplied by the user. The GitHub profile `https://github.com/Izhan-web` may be included. Do not invent an email address or LinkedIn URL. If no other contact details are available, keep the contact area minimal and omit unavailable links.

### 6. Footer
- `© 2026 Izhan. All rights reserved.`
- Keep it understated.

## Explicit exclusions
No projects or portfolio showcase, no project cards, no testimonials, no statistics, no services, no pricing, no blog, no résumé button, no newsletter, no fake terminal, no stock photography, and no placeholder content.

## Definition of done
The result is a complete, responsive one-page website with a clear introduction, working navigation, a restrained visual identity, useful real links only, and no unfinished or decorative-only controls.
